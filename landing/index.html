Why it exists

Other tools told me what was wrong.
None of them told me why.

I use English every day — emails, Slack, pull requests, Notion docs, you name it. And for years I kept making the same mistakes. Grammar tools would silently fix them, and I'd make them again the next day. I wasn't learning anything.

I wanted something that worked like a colleague doing a code review: here's what's wrong, here's why it's wrong, and here's how to write it correctly. That's what Teaching Mode is.

writerIAit has no backend — your text goes directly from your browser to whichever AI provider you choose. Nothing is stored or logged by writerIAit.

So I built it. And then I made it open source, because anyone writing in their second language deserves a tool like this.

How it works

01

Type normally

Write in any text field — Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Notion, or any website.

02

Trigger the check

Press Cmd + Shift + K on Mac or Ctrl + Shift + K on Windows.

03

Review the diff

Accept or skip each correction. Every change includes a plain-language reason.

Your text

Hi Sarah, I wanted to follow up in the email I sended last week about the proposal. Please let me know if you have any question. I am available for discuss anytime this week.
W
writerIAit Correct
×
sended → sent

"Send" is an irregular verb. Its simple past form is "sent", not "sended".

any question → any questions

After "any" in questions and negatives, English uses the plural form.

for discuss → to discuss

"Available" takes a to-infinitive, not a preposition + bare verb. Common Spanish/Portuguese transfer error.
